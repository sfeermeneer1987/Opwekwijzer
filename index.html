SKYGUARD

TEST BUILD · THE LIVING CITY

A whole city to protect: downtown, the harbor and its bridge, the industrial zone, the suburbs and the park. Calls for help come in while you roam: fires, getaways, robberies, falling people, meteors, a giant robot and more. Every building can still be smashed.
LEFT THUMB
Run. In the air: steer, climb, dive. Let go to hover. Push past the ring to boost.
RIGHT THUMB
Drag to look and aim.
FLY
Double-tap the stick to take off. Double-tap again to land.
DASH
A short burst that smashes through things and dodges shots. Uses the orange meter.
POWER
Big right button: tap to fire, hold to beam or spray, drag while holding to aim. The small buttons switch power.
PEOPLE
Walk up to anyone: fist bump, selfie, talk, or carry them. Thugs on the street? The action button becomes PUNCH; DASH when you see the red "!" to counter.
VIEW
Land and you see through your own eyes; take off and the camera pulls back. The ◉ button (top left) switches any time.
CALLS
Follow the orange marker. Finish calls for XP, upgrades and safer districts. Tap the map (top left) to see everything.
